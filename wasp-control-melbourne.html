Where wasps nest

Wasps build nests in roof cavities, wall voids, sheds, under eaves and in gardens. Nests can grow over a season, and activity usually increases in warmer months. Wasps can sting repeatedly when disturbed, so nests should not be approached.

Signs of a wasp nest

  • Steady traffic in and out of one spot, such as a roof gap or wall void
  • A grey papery nest under eaves, in sheds or in garden shrubs
  • Increased activity around food, drinks and bins

How we treat wasp nests

1. Species check

We confirm the wasp species, because the right approach depends on the type and location of the nest.

2. Nest assessment

We assess the nest’s location and size from a safe distance, and check whether it’s near doors, play areas or a bedroom.

3. Written quote

4. Treatment

Treatment is carried out with protective equipment, using APVMA-registered products applied according to the label.

5. Advice to stop re-nesting

We advise on food and bin management and on sealing gaps that wasps use to nest.
