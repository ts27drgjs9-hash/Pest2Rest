Wasp or bee? Here’s how to tell.

The difference matters: wasps and bees are handled differently, so the right answer starts with identifying the insect.

Wasps

  • Smooth, shiny body with a narrow waist
  • Can sting more than once
  • Often come to food, drinks and bins

Honey bees

  • Furry, rounder body, often with pollen on the back legs
  • A barbed sting that stays behind, so a honey bee usually dies after stinging
  • Mostly visit flowers rather than food scraps

Can you remove a possum from my roof?

Possums are protected under the Wildlife Act 1975 in Victoria. We do not trap, harm or relocate them. We seal entry points so they cannot get back in, and if one is trapped inside we refer you to a licensed wildlife controller.

Do you relocate bee swarms?

We identify whether it is a swarm or an established hive. Where a beekeeper can collect the colony, we help arrange it. If collection is not possible, we discuss treatment with you before anything is done.

Are your products APVMA registered?

We use APVMA-registered products and apply them according to the label. The APVMA registers products, so that is the accurate way to describe our chemicals.

Is it safe for kids and pets?

When we follow the label, yes. We tell you how long to keep children and pets away from treated areas, lock bait stations and keep products away from food and water.
