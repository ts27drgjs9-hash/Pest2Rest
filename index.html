How we handle possums, under Victorian law

Possums are protected under the Wildlife Act 1975. It is illegal to harm them or interfere with them without authority, and relocating a possum is not permitted. Our work is built around that.

1. Inspection

We find out which possum it is, where it is getting in, and whether animals or young are inside before anything else happens.

2. Lawful exclusion

Once the roof space is clear, we seal entry points with durable mesh and metal flashing so possums can’t get back in. Work is timed around breeding and dependent young.

3. Licensed referral

If a possum is trapped inside, we don’t trap, harm or move it ourselves. We refer you to a licensed wildlife controller, and any lethal control needs an Authority to Control Wildlife from DEECA.

Safety and precautions

Every treatment follows the product label and our safety procedures, so your family, pets and property stay protected.

While we treat

  • Products are used only as directed on the label, at the lowest effective rate
  • Protective equipment is worn for every job
  • Spraying is kept away from food preparation areas, water sources and play areas
  • Bait stations are locked or placed out of reach of children and pets
  • We warn occupants and keep areas closed off until treated surfaces are dry

After we leave

  • We tell you how long to keep kids and pets away and when to clean surfaces
  • Unused product and waste are taken away and disposed of as the label requires
  • We leave a written record of what was used and where
  • Follow-up visits are offered to check results

How we look after your property

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Call or message us.

How much does a visit cost?

Possum management starts from $240 and rodent treatment for mice and rats is $280. These are starting prices. We confirm the exact quote after inspection, before any work starts.

Do you offer a free inspection?

Yes. Your second job includes a free inspection.

Can you remove a possum from my roof?

Possums are protected under the Wildlife Act 1975 in Victoria. We don’t trap, harm or relocate them. We seal entry points so they can’t get back in, and if one is trapped inside we refer you to a licensed wildlife controller.

Is it safe for my kids and pets?

Yes, when we follow the label. We tell you how long to keep children and pets away from treated areas, lock bait stations and keep products away from food and water.

Are white-tailed spiders dangerous?

White-tailed spiders can give a painful bite that may swell or itch. Wash the bite, use a cold pack, and see a doctor if pain spreads, you feel unwell or you have a known allergy. Call 000 for severe reactions.

How long does treatment take to work?

Many pests start to decline within days, but some, like rodents and possums, can take longer. We schedule follow-up visits to check results.

Are your treatments safe for the environment?

We use products as directed on the label, use the lowest effective rate, and keep product away from water and wildlife where possible.

Do I need to be home?

It helps, so we can explain the work and check access. If you can’t be there, tell us in the enquiry form and we’ll discuss how to arrange it.
