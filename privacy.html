Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

Pest2Rest (ABN 17 815 994 053) provides pest control services in Melbourne, including wasps, bees, spiders, cockroaches, ants, possums and rats and mice. You can contact us at info@pest2rest.com.au or on +61 401 042 325.

What information we collect

  • Enquiry details: your name, phone number, suburb or address, the service you need and any message you add. The form on this website opens WhatsApp with these details filled in. Your enquiry is only sent when you press send in WhatsApp.
  • Calls and messages: when you call or message us, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us.
  • Website usage and advertising measurement: this website uses Google tags to measure visits and to count taps on our phone number. These tools may set cookies and collect information such as the page you visited and the device you used.
  • Fonts: this website loads fonts from Google, which may record basic connection information.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote and provide our services.
  • To keep records of work carried out, including warranty claims.
  • To measure how well our website and advertising perform, so we can improve them.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share information with

  • WhatsApp (operated by Meta), when you send an enquiry through it.
  • Google, for website measurement and Google Ads conversion tracking.
  • Our service providers, such as the platform that hosts this website, where needed to keep it running.

We only share information where it's needed for these purposes, or where the law requires it.

Cookies

Google's tools may place cookies on your device to measure visits and advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage ad personalisation through your Google account. Blocking cookies may affect how some parts of the website work, but you can still call or message us directly.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and job details for as long as needed to respond to you, provide our services and meet any legal or record-keeping obligations. When it's no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we need to keep it for legal reasons. Contact us at info@pest2rest.com.au.

If you're not satisfied with how we handle your information, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
